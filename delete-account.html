Delete Your Forgeborn Account

You can request deletion of your Forgeborn account and all associated data at any time.

How to request deletion

Email hello@playforgeborn.com from the email address linked to your Forgeborn account, with the subject line "Delete my account."

Include your in-app username or character name if you know it, this helps us find your account faster.

We will confirm your identity and delete your account within 30 days of your request.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your character data, including class, level, XP and stats
  • Your workout history and logbook entries
  • Your story progress
  • Your Aldric chat history

What may be retained

  • Records required for tax, accounting or legal compliance, such as subscription payment records, which may be retained for a limited period after deletion as required by law
  • Anonymised or aggregated data that can no longer be linked back to you

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active Pro subscription. Please cancel your subscription through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store before requesting account deletion, to avoid being charged after your account is removed.

Questions

If you have any questions about this process, contact us at hello@playforgeborn.com.
